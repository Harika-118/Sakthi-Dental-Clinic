COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Here are answers to some of the questions our patients ask most often.

What dental treatments do you provide? +

We provide dental cleaning, tooth filling, teeth extraction, dental implants, teeth bleaching and orthodontic treatment.

How can I book a dental appointment? +

You can book an appointment by contacting us through our Contact page or by calling the clinic directly.

What are the clinic timings? +

Our clinic is open from 9 AM to 7 PM, Monday to Sunday.

Do you provide dental treatment for children? +

Yes. We provide family-friendly dental care and treatment options for children.

Is parking available at the clinic? +

Yes. Hassle-free parking is available for patients visiting the clinic.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible? +

Yes. Wheelchair access is available to help patients visit the clinic comfortably.

Where is Sakthi Dental Clinic located? +

We are located at B2/8, SBM Layout, Anthivadi, Hosur, Tamil Nadu 635109, India.

How can I contact the clinic? +

You can contact us by phone, email or through the enquiry form on our Contact page.
